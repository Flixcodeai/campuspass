CP CampusPass Solana Devnet

Event tickets for student initiatives, settled on Solana.

Pay with any Solana wallet via Solana Pay. Your payment is your ticket: verifiable on-chain, impossible to fake, no PayPal fees, no Excel lists.

How it works

  1. Choose an event and scan the Solana Pay QR (Phantom / Solflare mobile) or pay with the Phantom browser extension.
  2. The payment carries a unique reference key and an on-chain memo with the event ID.
  3. CampusPass finds the transaction on-chain and issues your QR ticket.
  4. At the door, the organizer scans the ticket. CampusPass verifies amount, recipient and event directly on the blockchain.

Need devnet SOL? Use faucet.solana.com. In Phantom enable Settings → Developer Settings → Testnet Mode (Solana Devnet).
